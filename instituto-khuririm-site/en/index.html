OUR STORY

A dream born in the heart of God.

Every great transformation begins with a purpose.

The story of Instituto Khuririm has its roots in 1998, when the desire arose to use sport as a tool for social transformation.

What began as a dream placed by God in the heart of its founder became a mission: to offer opportunities to children and teenagers, especially those facing social vulnerability.

Along the way, we learned that teaching goes far beyond a sports technique.

It is welcoming. It is guiding. It is teaching respect. It is developing discipline. It is helping a child believe in themselves.

Today, Instituto Khuririm carries this purpose forward, expanding its work through sport, education, social-emotional development and specialized support.

Our mission is not only to train athletes. It is to help shape human beings with principles, values and opportunities for the future.

“This brand carries my story and my confidence in what we will still build together.”

Anderson Khuririm · founder, Jiu-Jitsu black-belt instructor

MISSION AND VISION

Why we exist and where we want to go.

At Instituto Khuririm, we use sport, education and social-emotional development as tools to transform realities, strengthen principles and values, and open new paths for children and teenagers.

More than teaching a sport, we want to prepare people for the challenges of life.

Our mission

To promote the human development and social transformation of children and teenagers through sport, education and the strengthening of social-emotional skills, providing opportunities for personal growth and for building a better future.

Our vision

To be a reference institution in human development and sports education, recognized for its ability to transform realities, strengthen families and create positive social impact in the communities where it works.

TRIBUTE

When someone believes in a dream.

A friendship born on the mat. A partnership built on purpose.

Cristiano Caporezzo
Sponsor and supporter of Instituto Khuririm

Some partnerships do not begin with big projects, but with encounters that God prepares along our journey.

In 2019, when Cristiano Caporezzo was still preparing to run for a seat on the Uberlândia City Council, his story crossed paths with Anderson Khuririm's through jiu-jitsu.

Khuririm was his son's instructor, and from that relationship a bond of trust was born.

By then, Khuririm had already dedicated years of his life to working with children through sport. During a conversation, he shared with Caporezzo a dream he carried in his heart: to expand this work and reach more and more children and teenagers, offering opportunities for transformation through sport, education and the shaping of principles and values.

Caporezzo listened to that dream and immediately committed to helping make it come true.

Since then, he has become an important supporter and sponsor of the project, helping to strengthen this mission.

A message from the founder

“In 2019, I shared with Caporezzo a dream that God had placed in my heart many years before.

At that time, he was still building his political career, but he did not hesitate to offer his help.

Time has passed, much has changed, but the importance of that conversation remains.

I am grateful to God for the people He places in our path, and to Caporezzo for believing in this purpose.

My wish is that what began as a dream may reach thousands of children all across Brazil.

May our journey be remembered not for titles or personal achievements, but for the lives we were able to transform.”

Anderson Khuririm · Founder of Instituto Khuririm

OUR PROGRAMS

Five paths. One single purpose: transforming lives.

Jiu-jitsu: sport and character building

Jiu-jitsu is one of the main tools of transformation used by the Institute.

Through sport, children and teenagers develop discipline, respect, self-control, confidence, responsibility and the ability to overcome challenges.

Our work is not limited to sports training. We seek to teach values that will stay with our students for life.

Social-emotional development

We believe emotional development is as important as physical and intellectual development.

That is why we work on skills such as self-awareness, empathy, respect, coexistence, communication, responsibility and emotional intelligence.

Our goal is to help children and teenagers learn to recognize their emotions, build healthy relationships and face everyday challenges.

English: knowledge that opens doors

Access to education broadens horizons and creates possibilities.

Through English language teaching, we seek to offer new opportunities for learning, cultural development and preparation for the future.

Spanish: broadening horizons

Learning a new language also means discovering new cultures and expanding the possibilities of communication.

Spanish language activities contribute to the educational and cultural development of participants.

Psychological support

With the support of licensed professionals, we seek to offer care, guidance and psychological support, contributing to the emotional well-being and healthy development of participants.

We understand that caring for emotions is also part of building a better future.
